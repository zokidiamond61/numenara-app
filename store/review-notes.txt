1. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Numenara is a numerology app by numerologist Nan Numero (Barbara Tot, the account holder). From a name and date of birth it calculates a personal numerology profile (Pythagorean system and Chaldean Numerology) and explains each number in plain language. It helps adults interested in numerology and self-reflection understand their character, strengths and life cycles, and gives new daily wisdom, advice and a ritual every day. Audience: adults (13+) who speak English or Serbian. The app is for self-reflection and entertainment; it gives no medical, legal or financial advice.

2. HOW TO USE / ACCESS FEATURES
No account, login or registration exists. All data is stored only on the device.
- Launch the app, tap Begin, enter any first name, last name and birth date (time is optional), tap Reveal.
- Free: My numbers tab (core numbers, tap any number for its meaning), Cycles tab, Daily tab.
- Paid: menu (top left) lists Pro sections (Karma, Energy, Home numerology, Chaldean details, Calendar, Synastry). Opening one shows the subscription screen with auto-renewable subscriptions: each section 9.99 USD / month, Complete Pro (everything, no ads) 59.99 USD / 3 months. Restore purchases and Manage subscriptions are on the same screen; Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are linked there.
- To review all Pro content without purchasing: open the side menu (top left), tap "Gift code" and enter NN-U58QEB (also available as "Have a gift code?" on the subscription screen). This unlocks Complete Pro on this device.
- Language: flag switch (top right) changes between English and Serbian.

3. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) for subscriptions.
- Google AdMob for banner ads in the free version (with Google's consent form where required and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are hosted on GitHub Pages.
No backend server, no user accounts, no AI services, no external data providers. All calculations run locally in the app.

4. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions where it is available (China mainland is excluded from distribution). Only the ads may differ by region, and users in the EEA/UK see the consent form before personalized ads.
