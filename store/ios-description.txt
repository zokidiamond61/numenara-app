Numenara is a numerology app created with numerologist Nan Numero. Enter your name and date of birth and discover the numbers that shape your character, your path and your days.

YOUR PYTHAGOREAN CODE
Your three-digit code – biorhythm, generation and personal number – and what it says about your character, strengths and shadow sides.

BIRTH DATE NUMBERS
Biorhythm, generation, personal number, birthday, maturity and degree, each explained clearly and simply.

CHALDEAN NUMEROLOGY – YOUR NAME
The hidden energy of your name: character number, full personal expression, soul urge and personality.

KARMA
Your karmic squares and the lessons you brought into this life.

CYCLES
Your code for today, this month and this year, and the energy each one brings.

COMPATIBILITY & SYNASTRY
Add your partner, family or friends and see how your numbers work together – love, communication, family, money and trust.

COLOURS, CRYSTALS & FILMS
Your lucky colours, a guide to 34 crystals with photos, and films that match your numbers.

EVERY DAY
Affirmation of the day, wisdom of the day, plus daily advice, focus and a small ritual – a new one for every day of the year.

NUMENARA PRO (OPTIONAL)
Basic numbers are always free. Subscribe only to what you want:
• a single Pro section – monthly auto-renewing subscription
• Complete Pro – all sections, no ads – auto-renews every 3 months
Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel your subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account needed. Your names and dates stay only on your phone.
